Data-handling controls

  • Primary records are stored in local SQLite; images remain in local audit folders under internal IDs.
  • Accounts are available after consent. Administrators may suspend abusive accounts but do not approve every registration.
  • Access, review changes, image views, exports and verified deletions are logged.
  • The configured default retention period is seven years; earlier deletion remains appropriate when the purpose ends and no legal requirement applies.
  • Exports are derived copies and must be protected by the person downloading them.
